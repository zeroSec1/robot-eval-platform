# Buyer economics & sales research (agent report, 2026-08-02)

## 1. AMR purchase and pilot economics
- AMR unit prices $20,000-$150,000 per unit; collaborative picking AMRs $25,000-$50,000; transport AMRs $30,000-$80,000; autonomous forklifts $60,000-$100,000. Source: Qviro Blog 2025, https://qviro.com/blog/cost-of-autonomous-mobile-robots/ . SECONDHAND.
- RaaS general range $2,000-$8,000 per robot/month; small pilot of 3 AMRs ~$120,000-$250,000 all-in first year; 8-12 robot fleet ~$350,000-$750,000 first year; integration/WMS connectors $15,000-$100,000. Source: PickTheRobot (Jul 16, 2026, figures labeled "illustrative"), https://picktherobot.com/blog/warehouse-robot-cost-2026 . SECONDHAND.
- Locus Robotics: ~$2,000-$4,000 per robot/month (no primary price list; Locus does not publish pricing). Source: ArticSledge https://www.articsledge.com/post/robots-as-a-service-raas . SECONDHAND. (checkthat.ai claims "$35k/robot + $2k/month" - unverified, low quality.)
- inVia "Pay-Per-Productivity" RaaS "as low as $4 an hour" (vendor marketing, https://inviarobotics.com/robotics-as-a-service/); aggregator: inVia picking $2,200-$3,200/mo 12-24mo terms; Fetch (Zebra) transport $2,000-$3,000/mo 12-36mo. Source: https://robotomated.com/learn/market/robotics-as-a-service-raas-guide . SECONDHAND.
- Pilot budgets: $50K-$150K typical starter pilot (2-5 AMRs, single zone); $100K-$250K mid-size; phased deployment 18-24 months. Source: Robotomated 2026, https://robotomated.com/learn/cost/warehouse-automation-budget-guide . SECONDHAND. No named-vendor published pilot price sheet exists.

## 2. US warehouse labor (BLS API pulls, retrieved Aug 1 2026 - DIRECT)
- Avg hourly earnings, Warehousing & Storage (NAICS 493): $26.21 (Dec 2025), $26.66 (May 2026); production/nonsupervisory $25.25 / $25.92. BLS CES series CES4349300003 / CES4349300008. DIRECT.
- Avg weekly wage private warehousing: $1,074 Q1 2025, +4.6% YoY. BLS QCEW NAICS 493. DIRECT.
- JOLTS (Transportation/Warehousing/Utilities, no warehousing-only series exists): monthly total separations 4.0-4.7%/mo early 2026 (May 2026: 4.7%, quits 2.6%). 2025 total separations 3.466M vs ~6.6M sector jobs = annual separations ~ half the workforce. BLS series JTS480099000000000TSR/QUR/TSL + CES4300000001. DIRECT (annualization = agent calculation).
- The cited "~49% annual warehouse turnover" (2022) is an industry derivation, BLS publishes no warehousing-only series. Source roundup: https://www.opensend.com/post/warehouse-turnover-rate-statistics . SECONDHAND.
- Job openings Transportation/Warehousing/Utilities: 298,000 (May 2026), 341,000 (Apr 2026). BLS JOLTS JTS480099000000000JOL. DIRECT.
- MHI 2025 Annual Industry Report: hiring/retention "extremely challenging" for 52%; talent shortage 45%. Via https://www.sellerscommerce.com/blog/warehouse-automation-statistics/ . SECONDHAND.
- Instawork State of Warehouse Labor 2025: 50% of leaders said staffing easier in 2025 than 2024 (up from 38%) - shortage easing but top challenge. https://www.supplychain247.com/article/warehouse-labor-shortages-instawork-report . SECONDHAND.
- Peerless Research Group 2026 Outlook (MMH, Mar 4 2026, 103 respondents, article read in full): labor availability "very important" for 57%. https://www.mmh.com/article/2026_outlook_survey_signs_of_caution_but_automation_marches_on . DIRECT.

## 4. Buyer pool
- 23,075 private US Warehousing & Storage establishments Q1 2025, ~1.87-1.95M employees (Jan 2025: 1,946,634). BLS QCEW. DIRECT. (Caveat: retailer/manufacturer-operated warehouses classified elsewhere; true building count higher. CBRE publishes no total count.)
- ~72,937 US 3PL businesses (IBISWorld 2024, broad def); 26,216 active freight brokerages (FMCSA Aug 2024). Via https://redstagfulfillment.com/how-many-3pls-are-there/ . SECONDHAND.
- Robot adoption (PRG 2026 Outlook, MMH, DIRECT): 16% currently use AMRs/AGVs (up from 10% prior year); 27% plan to evaluate in 1-2 years; 21% use industrial robots (up from 13%).
- Interact Analysis: by 2030 only 13% of warehouses will have deployed at least one fulfillment AMR. Via FreightWaves https://www.freightwaves.com/news/warehouse-automation-surging-ahead-despite-predicted-slowdown . SECONDHAND.
- Interact Analysis global survey of 300 mobile-robot buyers (Jul 30 2026, article read): 59% already use some mobile automation (skews large). https://www.automatedwarehouseonline.com/interact-analysis-asks-what-customers-want-from-mobile-robots-in-global-survey/ . DIRECT.

## 5. ROI / payback expectations
- Interact Analysis 300-buyer survey (Jul 30 2026, quotes verified): 80% expect ROI within 3 years, most 18mo-3yr; 3PLs expect fastest payback; buyers expect to SEE the system in action (demo/reference site) before purchase. Same Automated Warehouse article. DIRECT. Best-sourced ROI stat.
- 2024 PRG survey via VNDLY: SMB payback avg 18-24mo; hardware automation 24-36mo; software <12mo. https://www.vndly.io/blog/warehouse-automation-roi-smb-2026 . SECONDHAND.
- "Most operations target 12-24mo payback"; AMR picking 18-24mo; conveyor 24-36mo; AS/RS 36-60mo (MHI attribution unverified). https://reemanbot.com/posts/warehouse-automation-payback-period-realistic-timelines-by-operation-type . SECONDHAND.
